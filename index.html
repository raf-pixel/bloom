Camera help

Keep one hand in view, palm facing the camera. Open all four fingers to bloom; curl them into a loose fist to close. Use the Colour button to change colour. Fully open your hand for a pollen burst. Use a well-lit room. If your hand leaves the frame, the flower holds its last position.

Moving the slider stops the camera and returns to manual control. Camera mode needs internet to download Google's MediaPipe tracking software and model. After loading, hand detection runs locally. No microphone access is requested.

Download this file and open it in Chrome or Edge. If your browser blocks camera access from a local file, use the local launcher included in the ZIP. The slider works without a camera or internet.

Tracking powered by Google MediaPipe. This is an interactive artwork, not a botanical simulation.
